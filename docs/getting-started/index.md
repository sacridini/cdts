# Get Started

<p class="lead">New to CDTS, or to satellite time series in general? These pages take you from installation to your first map, and explain the handful of ideas that every tutorial builds on.</p>

<div class="steps" markdown>

<div markdown>
**[Install CDTS](installation.md)**

One `pip install`. Pre-built wheels, no compiler needed.
</div>

<div markdown>
**[Run the quickstart](quickstart.md)**

Detect two clearings in a synthetic forest and map when they happened. Takes about five minutes.
</div>

<div markdown>
**[Learn the core concepts](concepts.md)**

Array shapes, dates, scale factors and the three ways to call every algorithm.
</div>

<div markdown>
**[Choose an algorithm](choosing-an-algorithm.md)**

Match your question to the right method, then follow its tutorial.
</div>
